**What Matters When Building Vision-Language Models?**

This paper investigates the design choices made in vision-language models (VLMs) and their impact on performance. The authors argue that many design choices are not justified experimentally, making it difficult to identify which choices improve model performance.

**Key Findings:**

1. **Pre-trained backbones are crucial**: The quality of the language model backbone has a higher impact on the performance of the final VLM than the quality of the vision backbone.
2. **Fully autoregressive architecture outperforms cross-attention**: When training the unimodal backbones, the fully autoregressive architecture outperforms the cross-attention architecture, even though the latter has more parameters.
3. **Reducing the number of visual tokens improves efficiency**: Using a perceiver resampler to reduce the number of visual tokens significantly improves compute efficiency at training and inference while improving performance on downstream tasks.
4. **Adapting vision encoders to preserve aspect ratio and resolution**: Adapting a vision encoder pre-trained on fixed-size square images to preserve images' original aspect ratio and resolution does not degrade performance while speeding up training and inference and reducing memory.
5. **Splitting images into sub-images trades compute for performance**: Splitting images into sub-images during training allows trading compute efficiency for more performance during inference.

**Idefics2: A New Vision-Language Model**

The authors train Idefics2, an open 8B parameters vision-language model, using a combination of pre-training and instruction fine-tuning. Idefics2 achieves state-of-the-art performance in its size category on various benchmarks while being more efficient at inference.

**Key Contributions:**

1. **New dataset**: The authors create a new dataset, The Cauldron, which includes 50 vision-language datasets covering a wide range of tasks.
2. **Improved performance**: Idefics2 outperforms current state-of-the-art VLMs in its size category on various benchmarks.
3. **Efficient inference**: Idefics2 is more efficient at inference than other VLMs, making it a more practical choice for real-world applications.

**Limitations and Future Work:**

1. **Security risks**: The authors identify security risks associated with VLMs, including the potential for generating malicious content.
2. **Jail-breaking**: The authors highlight the need for further research on jail-breaking VLMs, which can circumvent cautionary prompts and generate problematic content.

Overall, this paper provides valuable insights into the design choices made in VLMs and their impact on performance. The authors' contributions, including the creation of a new dataset and the development of a new vision-language model, will help advance the field of VLMs.
